About the workshop

Millbrook Cycle Works opened in 2016 as a two-person repair stand and now has four mechanics. It is entirely fictional.

How we work

Every bike gets the same first step: a written inspection checklist covering brakes, drivetrain, wheels, headset and bolts. The quote comes from that checklist, so you can see what we found before you decide what to fix.

We use a torque wrench on every bolt that has a published torque value, and we measure chain wear with a gauge instead of judging it by eye.

What we work on

Road, gravel, mountain and commuter bikes, plus e-bikes and cargo bikes. We do not service suspension forks or rear shocks in house, and we will tell you so at the quote stage.

Meet the team

The workshop is run by four mechanics who each trained on different disciplines, from road racing to touring. If you have a particular bike in mind, get in touch before you drop it off.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an appointment?

No. You can drop a bike off any time we are open. For full services in busy spring weeks, booking ahead through the contact page shortens the wait.

Do you work on e-bikes?

Yes, for brakes, wheels, drivetrain and general maintenance. We do not repair motors, batteries or controllers, and we will refer you to the manufacturer if the fault is electrical.

Can I wait while you work on my bike?

For puncture repairs and quick adjustments, yes. Anything longer is a drop-off job, and the price list shows typical turnaround for each service.

Is the work guaranteed?

Labour carries a 90-day guarantee. Parts carry the manufacturer's own warranty.

Do you sell new bikes?

No. We repair and maintain bikes and sell only the parts we fit during a repair.
